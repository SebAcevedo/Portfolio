The brief

Promote local programming and news to English- and Spanish-speaking audiences across television, digital and social, on broadcast deadlines.

My role

At Univision I led creative development for campaigns aimed at Hispanic audiences, mentored junior producers and established editorial review processes to improve consistency across campaigns.

At NBCUniversal I developed and produced campaigns for local and national audiences with editorial, marketing and production teams, from concept through delivery.

The decisions

  • Raise the floor, not just the ceiling. A shared editorial review process meant quality held across the whole team, not only on my own spots.
  • Write for the audience, then the channel. Each Spanish-language campaign was developed for its audience first, then adapted for television, digital and social.
  • Story before the edit. I shaped structure, pacing and tone in development, so post-production refined the idea instead of finding it.

Outcome

  • Two Emmy Awards, 2023. Campaigns I produced at NBCUniversal won in News Promotion Image – Single Spot and Promotion Image – Campaign.
  • CBA Awards of Excellence, 2023: Single Spot and Campaign.
