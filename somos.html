We are Lafayette

Somos Lafayette — launch film · English subtitles

The brief

The City of Lafayette wanted to expand its outreach to Spanish-speaking residents. It needed a name, a look and a story that this community would recognize as its own.

My role

I created the concept, set the creative direction and directed the launch film. I designed the Somos Lafayette logo and identity, and brought it to community events.

The decisions

  • Residents tell the story. The launch film is built from residents speaking in their own words: "We are an accent, a melody." "We are the connection between generations." It closes on one line: "We are Lafayette."
  • Show everyday Lafayette. Between the voices, the film moves through the places people actually share: a festival, a mural, the pool, a community garden, chalk art on the street.
  • A Spanish name for the whole city. "Somos" means "we are." The logo sets it behind Lafayette in blue, and the film carries English subtitles, so the identity speaks to both languages.
  • Part of the City's brand system. Somos Lafayette lives inside the Lafayette Brand Hub as a sub-brand, next to the City's own identity.
  • Take it where people are. The identity went beyond the screen, to community events.

Outcome

Somos Lafayette launched in 2024 as the City's bilingual outreach identity.
